Our Story

Like our namesake,
the holy basil.

In Indian households, a tulsi plant stands near every doorway — the revered holy basil, a quiet emblem of purity, wellness, and welcome. We took the name because food should do the same things: nourish, gather, and feel like home.

Our kitchen draws from every region of India, from Kashmiri slow-cooked lamb to the coconut-warm curries of Kerala. Spices are hand-ground the morning of service, breads pulled from the tandoor to order, and every curry starts — as they have for centuries — with onion and oil.

Philosophy

Three roots
we grow from.

Like the tulsi plant — said to purify the air around it — we want every plate to leave the room a little better than it found it.

01

Purity

Whole spices ground the morning of service. No shortcuts, no powders, no pre-made sauces. Every curry starts with onion and oil.

02

Wellness

More than half the menu is plant-forward. We cook with ghee, cold-pressed oils, and vegetables from nearby farms when the season allows.

03

Hospitality

Indian dining has always been about the table, not the plate. Come for the lamb chops, stay for the chai, leave like family.
